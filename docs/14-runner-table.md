## Read this before reading the table

**Survivorship.** These are pools an aggregator lists *today*. A token that crossed the threshold and collapsed into obscurity is absent — not because it did not happen, but because nothing tracks it now. The multiples below are therefore an optimistic sample. This table answers *what did the ones that made it do*, not *what happens if you buy at $250k*.

**Reconstructed cap.** Supply is inferred as `fdv_now / price_now` and assumed constant. A token that minted after launch has its early cap overstated, which makes its crossing look earlier and its multiple smaller. This is FDV, not circulating.

**Peak vs realisable.** *Peak* is the highest close. *Realisable* is the highest price at or above which a tenth of the post-crossing volume traded — a price the market demonstrably absorbed size at. Where the two diverge sharply, the peak was a wick nobody could have sold into.
